## 🏛 About the Conference

> 🌐 **Official Live Portal:** [**https://iessd26.com/**](https://iessd26.com/) — Access full conference tracks, keynote schedules, paper templates, author guidelines, and online registration.

**IESSD'26** is a prestigious multi-track international conference jointly organized by the **Department of Mechanical Engineering** and the **Department of Civil Engineering** at **Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering & Technology (SLIET)**, Longowal, Punjab, India.

Hosted live on the web at [**https://iessd26.com/**](https://iessd26.com/), the conference serves as a global forum uniting leading academic scientists, researchers, scholars, and industry professionals to deliberate on pioneering advancements and address global challenges in engineering. With a strong commitment to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), IESSD'26 bridges theoretical discoveries with scalable, eco-friendly, and commercially viable solutions across smart manufacturing, intelligent robotics, and sustainable infrastructure.

### Organizing Institutions & Collaborations
- **Host Institution:** Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering & Technology (SLIET), Longowal (Deemed-to-be-University under MoE, Govt. of India)
- **Host Departments:** Department of Mechanical Engineering & Department of Civil Engineering
- **In Association with:**
  - The Institution of Engineers (India) [IEI]
  - Institution of Civil Engineers (India) [ICE]
  - Atmanirbhar Bharat Initiative

## ✨ Key Highlights

- **Multi-Track Scientific Discourse:** 3 dedicated technical tracks spanning modern engineering frontiers.
- **Hybrid Format:** In-person presentations at the SLIET campus with synchronous virtual sessions for international delegates.

## 🔬 Conference Tracks & Topics

### Track 1: Innovations in Smart Manufacturing
*Department of Mechanical Engineering*

- Smart Materials & Their Manufacturing
- Material Recycling, Characterization & Circular Economy
- Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) in Manufacturing
- Additive Manufacturing (3D/4D Printing) & Rapid Prototyping
- Polymers, Composites & Nanomaterials in Modern Processes
- Environmental Impact Assessment & Sustainable Production
- Process Optimization & Advanced Machining
- Surface Engineering Science & Coatings Technology
- Impact of Post-Processing on Manufactured Components
- Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) of Manufacturing Systems

### Track 2: Intelligent Robotics & Automation for Sustainable Systems
*Department of Mechanical Engineering*

- Intelligent Automation in Production Systems
- Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs) & Collaborative Robots (Cobots)
- AI & ML Architectures for Edge Robotics
- Intelligent Control Systems & Multi-Objective Optimization
- Computer Vision, Perception & Multi-Sensor Fusion
- Simulation & Digital Twin Modeling of Sustainable Systems
- Automatic Digitization & Cyber-Physical Systems
- Quantum Computing Applications in Engineering
- Innovations in Electric Vehicles (EV) & Battery Management
- Drone Technology, UAVs & Aerial Inspection

### Track 3: Innovations and Sustainability in Civil Engineering
*Department of Civil Engineering*

- Sustainable & Low-Carbon Construction Materials
- Smart Construction Techniques & Building Information Modeling (BIM)
- Energy-Efficient Systems & Renewable Energy Technologies
- Climate-Resilient & Disaster-Resistant Infrastructure
- Innovations in Urban Planning & Smart Cities
- Automation, Robotics & AI Systems in Civil Infrastructure
- Smart Transportation Systems & Sustainable Mobility
- Water Resource Engineering & Waste Management Innovations
- Structural Health Monitoring (SHM) & Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)
- Engineering Policy, Standards & Education for Sustainable Development

## 💻 About the Portal & Tech Stack

This conference portal is engineered as a modern, responsive single-page web application featuring high-performance animations, accessible components, and modular design.

### Technologies
- **Core Framework:** [React 18](https://react.dev/) + [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/)
- **Build Tool:** [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/) with SWC compiler
- **Styling:** [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) + custom design tokens
- **UI Components:** [Radix UI](https://www.radix-ui.com/) primitives styled via [shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com/)
- **Animations:** [Framer Motion](https://www.framer.com/motion/) with accessibility-aware `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance
- **Icons:** [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Routing:** [React Router v6](https://reactrouter.com/)
- **State & Query:** [TanStack React Query v5](https://tanstack.com/query)
- **Unit Testing:** [Vitest](https://vitest.dev/) with JSDOM and Testing Library
- **E2E Testing:** [Playwright](https://playwright.dev/)

## 🚀 Getting Started & Local Setup

### Prerequisites
- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) (version `18.x` or higher recommended)
- [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/) (bundled with Node.js)

### Installation

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Prakhyat05/IESSD-26.git
   cd IESSD-26
   ```

2. **Install dependencies:**
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Start the local development server:**
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
   The application will be accessible at `http://localhost:8080`.

4. **Build for production:**
   ```bash
   npm run build
   ```
   The compiled static bundle will be generated in the `dist/` directory, optimized and ready for deployment on GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify, or any static host.

5. **Preview production build locally:**
   ```bash
   npm run preview
   ```
